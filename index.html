🔒 Privacy Policy

Application: TracePath Studio | Effective Date: October 2026 | Status: Active & Fully Compliant

TracePath Studio operates on an uncompromising privacy principle: Zero personal data collected, zero remote analytics, zero third-party SDKs, and 100% on-device local storage. We never track, profile, monetize, or transmit your or your child’s data.

1. Commitment to Child Privacy & COPPA Compliance

TracePath Studio is intentionally engineered for early developmental learners, children ages 5 and under, neurodivergent students, and pediatric therapy clinics. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act), and GDPR-K.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or physical locations. Student nicknames (e.g., “Leo” or “Maya”) created by therapists or parents are stored exclusively within the device’s local sandboxed SwiftData database.
  • No Account Creation or Authentication: The application requires zero logins, usernames, passwords, or third-party federated identity providers (such as Google or Apple Sign-In).
  • Zero Camera, Microphone, or Biometric Access: The app never requests camera, microphone, contact list, or photo library permissions. Synthetic audio chimes are generated entirely in software using local audio synthesizers.

2. Apple “Made for Kids” Category Compliance (Guidelines 1.3 & 5.1.4)

TracePath Studio strictly adheres to Apple App Store Review Guidelines for the Kids Category:

  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: The application contains no banner ads, video reward ads, interstitial pop-ups, or affiliate marketing.
  • Zero Analytics and Tracking SDKs: We do not bundle Google AdMob, Google Firebase, Meta SDK, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, Unity, or any commercial telemetry engines. The app does not request AppTrackingTransparency permission because no cross-app or cross-device tracking occurs.
  • Protected Clinician & Parental Gate: Any access out of the child-facing studio — including Clinician Settings, Student Profile Management, StoreKit purchases, and PDF report exports — is secured behind a dual-action parental gate (requiring either a 2.0-second continuous two-finger hold or a 4-digit PIN verification). Children under 5 cannot inadvertently leave the app, make purchases, or export clinical records.
  • No Child-Accessible External Links: There are zero hyperlinks, web browsers, or WKWebView instances in the child tracing canvas.

3. 100% On-Device Architecture & Telemetry Data

During tracing exercises, the app measures fine-motor telemetry (including discrete Fréchet distance, Euclidean deviation corridor adherence, Apple Pencil pressure/tilt, and lift-off counts).

All telemetry computations and storage take place 100% on-device. Telemetry exists solely to render real-time haptic/visual feedback and to generate ISO A4 progress reports for occupational therapy and IEP reviews. No metrics are ever transmitted to remote servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

Because all data is stored locally in the application’s isolated sandbox:

  • Parents and therapists may delete individual learner profiles and drawing sessions at any time inside the protected Clinician Dashboard.
  • Deleting the TracePath application from your iOS device immediately and permanently purges all local databases, student records, and settings.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Step-by-step guides for clinical reporting, offline use, and Stage 2 progression

How do I export clinical PDF progress reports to the iPad or iPhone Files app? 📄

TracePath Studio generates professional, publication-grade ISO A4 PDF progress reports tailored for IEP meetings and occupational therapy reviews. Follow these steps to generate and save a report:

  1. 1
    Open Clinician Settings: From the main studio drawing view, tap the Settings / Clinician Gear icon located in the upper-right corner.
  2. 2
    Pass the Dual Parental Gate: Perform a simultaneous two-finger hold for 2.0 continuous seconds on the screen, or enter your 4-digit clinician PIN (default: 2468).
  3. 3
    Select Learner Profile: Choose the student or child whose session history you wish to review.
  4. 4
    Generate Clinical Report: Tap “Export Clinical Report (PDF)”. The app will immediately synthesize Fréchet accuracy, lift-offs, corner dwells, and visual synthesis metrics into an ISO A4 document.
  5. 5
    Save to Files App: When the native iOS Share Sheet appears, select “Save to Files”, choose your target folder (e.g., iCloud Drive > Documents > TracePath Reports), and tap Save. You can also directly AirDrop or print the report.

Note: Reports are generated entirely on-device; no data is ever uploaded to a remote server.

Does TracePath operate 100% offline without an internet connection? ✈️

Yes. TracePath Studio operates 100% offline at all times.

The entire application is completely self-contained. It requires zero Wi-Fi, zero cellular data, and zero cloud connectivity:

  • Zero Cloud Dependencies: Stroke interpolation, haptic synthesis, and Beery VMI curriculum milestones run via local on-device Swift engines.
  • Secure Therapy & School Environments: Perfect for hospital sensory rooms, clinical environments with strict HIPAA / privacy rules, and school Wi-Fi networks with high-security firewalls.
  • Travel Ready: Fully functional in Airplane Mode during travel, home visits, or remote therapy sessions.

How do I access and unlock Stage 2: Picture Builder? 🎨

TracePath is organized around two developmental visual-motor stages based on the Beery VMI curriculum:

  • Stage 1 (Pre-Writing Strokes): Masters the 9 foundational geometric primitives (Vertical, Horizontal, Circle, Cross, Square, Right/Left Diagonals, X, and Triangle).
  • Stage 2 (Picture Builder & Visual-Motor Synthesis): Transitions the learner from isolated strokes to constructing recognizable pictorial compositions using sequential primitives.

To access Stage 2:

  1. 1
    From the studio navigation carousel, tap the “Stage 2: Picture Builder” segment at the top of the canvas selector.
  2. 2
    Select from 4 progressive synthesis tiers:
    • Tier 1 (Simple Associations): Sun, Balloon, Person, Flower, Tree
    • Tier 2 (Spatial Intersections): House, Kite, Ice Cream Cone, Sailboat
    • Tier 3 (Multi-Vector Synthesis): Butterfly, Snail, Rain Cloud, Car
    • Tier 4 (Personal Name Formation): Dynamic name composition using pre-writing primitives
  3. 3
    Clinician Tip: In the Clinician Dashboard, you can choose between Linear Developmental Unlocking (requires completion of prerequisite strokes) or Open Studio Mode (unlocks all Stage 1 and Stage 2 shapes immediately for differentiated therapy).

What is the default PIN for the Therapist / Parental Gate? 🔑

The factory default Clinician PIN is 2468.

You do not need to memorize the PIN: you can also unlock the gate at any time by performing a 2-second continuous two-finger hold on the dual touch target pads. Clinicians and parents can customize or update this PIN at any time inside Clinician Settings.

Does TracePath support Apple Pencil and palm rejection? ✏️

Yes. TracePath Studio features active palm rejection and Apple Pencil integration:

  • Apple Pencil Stylus Mode: The canvas recognizes only the pencil tip, actively ignoring resting wrists, palms, and accidental finger touches. Captures natural force pressure and azimuth tilt.
  • Hand / Finger Mode: For younger toddlers or learners practicing finger-tracing. Employs a strict single-touch filter to ignore accidental secondary contacts.
  • Adaptive Hybrid: Automatically transitions between Apple Pencil and finger tracing without requiring manual mode toggles.

How do I configure High-Contrast CVI Mode or adjust motor tolerance corridors? 👁️

TracePath provides neurodiversity-informed accessibility adaptations:

  • Cortical Visual Impairment (CVI) Mode: Enables a pure black canvas (#000000) with high-chroma yellow and cyan guide corridors for learners needing high visual contrast.
  • Adjustable Tolerance Corridors:
    • High Tolerance (SEN Mode, ±35pt leeway): Accommodates motor tremors, ataxia, and dyspraxia.
    • Medium Tolerance (Standard, ±20pt leeway): Balanced for emerging pre-writers.
    • Precision Mode (Handwriting Prep, ±8pt leeway): Refines fine-motor pencil control.

Access these options at any time in the Clinician Dashboard > Accessibility & Sensory Preferences.
